append_audit_entry: [FIELD_ASSIGNMENT] MUST answer and write an AuditEntry whose recorded_at is instant, whose action, subject, principal, confirmation_id and erased_before are the given ones, whose outcome is the accepted member of = models.AuditOutcome, whose release_version is the imported RELEASE_VERSION constant and whose contract_version is the imported CONTRACT_VERSION constant.
erase_audit_entries_before: [VALIDATION_ERROR] MUST answer a Refusal with the invalid_request member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_VALUES constant names and one failure of the date_after_today member of = models.RefusalKind when before is later than today, removing nothing.
erase_audit_entries_before: [BEHAVIOR] MUST otherwise remove through delete_audit_entries_before on transaction every entry recorded before the start of before in UTC, answer ErasedEntries with the number it removed, and leave the counter untouched.
read_audit_entries: [DETERMINISM_OR_ORDERING] MUST answer every entry list_audit_entries gives on transaction, in ascending sequence number, and change nothing.

## service_access

identify_service: [SECURITY_BOUNDARY] MUST compare the UTF-8 bytes of presented_token through equal_in_constant_time with the bytes of every token give_service_tokens answers, asking for them at this call and keeping none, and answer an IdentifiedService of the service whose token is equal.
identify_service: [VALIDATION_ERROR] MUST answer a Refusal with the unauthenticated member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_AUTHENTICATION constant names and one failure of the no_valid_token member of = models.RefusalKind when presented_token is None or equals no token, an unknown token answered as a wrong one, without any count or limit.
service_may_call: [BEHAVIOR] MUST answer a ServicePermission whose may_call is true exactly when the imported SERVICE_RIGHTS constant holds a row of service and operation, and whose agent_authority is true exactly when may_call is true and service is the cabinet_kernel member of = models.ServiceName.
highest_class_of_service: [BEHAVIOR] MUST answer the highest_class of the row of service in the imported SERVICE_HIGHEST_CLASSES constant.

## owner_sessions

sign_in_owner: [BEHAVIOR] MUST, holding the module's one sign-in lock from its monotonic_reading to its decision, refuse without counting while a lockout holds, that is while the reading is less than the imported SIGN_IN_LOCKOUT_MINUTES constant, in nanoseconds of the monotonic reading, after the refusal that started it; otherwise compare the UTF-8 bytes of presented_credential with those of the credential give_owner_credential answers at this call through equal_in_constant_time, a missing credential counting as wrong.
sign_in_owner: [BEHAVIOR] MUST record a wrong credential's refusal at its reading and start a lockout at that refusal when _refused_sign_ins_since then answers the imported SIGN_IN_REFUSAL_LIMIT constant or more, never extending a lockout that holds; a right credential MUST clear every recorded refusal and make a new OwnerSession whose session_id and csrf_value come from make_secret_value, whose signed-in and last-request readings are the reading, not signed out, with no pending confirmation, no uploaded file and no upload numbered yet, leaving every other session open.
sign_in_owner: [RETURN_SHAPE] MUST answer SignedIn with the new session's session_id and csrf_value, or one Refusal with the unauthenticated member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_AUTHENTICATION constant names and one failure of the sign_in_refused member of = models.RefusalKind, the same whatever was wrong, never echoing the credential.
admit_owner_request: [SECURITY_BOUNDARY] MUST at arrival take one monotonic_reading, find the session whose session_id equals admission's through equal_in_constant_time, and refuse with the unauthenticated member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_AUTHENTICATION constant names and one failure of the no_owner_session member of = models.RefusalKind when there is none, it is signed out, or _session_reached_limit answers true at that reading; a session that passes MUST have its last-request reading raised to the arrival's reading unless a later one is already held, before any further check.
admit_owner_request: [VALIDATION_ERROR] MUST, for a change, refuse with the not_permitted member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_AUTHENTICATION constant names and one failure of the csrf_refused member of = models.RefusalKind when admission's csrf_value is not equal, through equal_in_constant_time, to the session's; a sign-out that passes MUST mark the session signed out at once, so a later arrival is refused.
admit_owner_request: [ORCHESTRATION] MUST then, for a request that touches an upload, wait for the instance's one import lock and afterwards take its place on the session's lock, for any other change take its place on the session's lock at once, and for a read take no lock; each lock MUST grant its waiters in the order they asked; it MUST run step with the session's session_id, answer what step answers and release every lock it took on every path, a raised failure included.
sign_out_owner: [BEHAVIOR] MUST end the session of session_id, which admit_owner_request admitted and marked signed out and whose locks it holds, removing it with its pending confirmation and its uploaded file from the module's sessions.
show_confirmation_step: [BEHAVIOR] MUST replace the session's pending confirmation with a new PendingConfirmation holding a new value from make_secret_value, the given action with its target and with shown_version as its stated version, the digest _confirmation_binding_digest gives for them and inputs, and the monotonic_reading it was shown at, and MUST answer that value; it MUST never keep inputs themselves.
take_confirmation: [VALIDATION_ERROR] MUST refuse with the confirmation_refused member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_AUTHENTICATION constant names: with the confirmation_not_pending member of = models.RefusalKind when the session holds no pending confirmation; with the confirmation_expired member, clearing the pending one whatever value is given, when the imported CONFIRMATION_VALID_MINUTES constant, in nanoseconds of the monotonic reading, has passed since it was shown; with the confirmation_other_value member, leaving the pending one as it is, when presented_value is None or not equal to it through equal_in_constant_time.
take_confirmation: [BEHAVIOR] MUST, once presented_value equals the pending value, clear the pending confirmation whatever follows, then answer ConfirmationTaken when action, target, stated_version and the digest _confirmation_binding_digest gives for them and inputs all equal the pending ones, and otherwise refuse with the confirmation_refused member of = models.RefusalCode at the stage the imported REFUSAL_STAGE_AUTHENTICATION constant names and one failure of the confirmation_bound_elsewhere member of = models.RefusalKind.
keep_uploaded_file: [BEHAVIOR] MUST number the upload one more than the session's last upload number, which sign-in leaves at no upload numbered, replace the session's uploaded file, one of the same bytes included, with an UploadedFile of content, file_digest and that number, keep it in the process's memory only, never in the store or on a disk, and answer the number.
read_uploaded_file: [RETURN_SHAPE] MUST answer the UploadedFile the session of session_id keeps, or None when it keeps none, and change nothing.
drop_uploaded_files: [BEHAVIOR] MUST remove the uploaded file of every owner session, keeping each session's last upload number, and change nothing else.
end_expired_sessions: [ORCHESTRATION] MUST take the instance's import lock, take one monotonic_reading, and for each session in the order of their sign-in take its place on that session's lock and end it, with its pending confirmation and uploaded file, when _session_reached_limit answers true at that one reading; it MUST release every lock on every path and answer how many sessions it ended.
_session_reached_limit: [BEHAVIOR] MUST answer true when the session is signed out, when reading is at or past its signed-in reading plus the imported OWNER_SESSION_MAX_HOURS constant, or when reading is at or past its last-request reading plus the imported OWNER_SESSION_IDLE_MINUTES constant, each duration in nanoseconds of the monotonic reading, and false otherwise.
_confirmation_binding_digest: [DETERMINISM_OR_ORDERING] MUST answer the hexadecimal text of a collision-resistant cryptographic digest of one unambiguous serialisation of the action, then the target, then the version, then every field of inputs in the order its model declares them, lists in the order given and an absent value told apart from an empty one, so that equal arguments always give equal digests and different ones different digests.
_refused_sign_ins_since: [BEHAVIOR] MUST answer how many recorded refused sign-ins have a reading less than the imported SIGN_IN_WINDOW_MINUTES constant, in nanoseconds of the monotonic reading, before reading.
